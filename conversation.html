An open invitation

The Guestbook.

Leave a thought, a question, or a disagreement. You do not have to agree with Markham to belong in this conversation.

Bring your questions.

Have you encountered an idea that changes how you understand experience, mind, or identity? Found an argument that challenges something here? Noticed a contradiction? We would like to hear it.

Contributions can be philosophical, scientific, personal, or playful. Curiosity and care matter more than credentials. Challenge ideas freely; treat people with care.

How to contribute.

The guestbook currently receives contributions by email. The link below opens your email app; you can also write directly to Robert@markhamideas.org. There is no account to create and no newsletter subscription.

Include the conversation title if you are responding to a particular piece, or simply bring an idea of your own. If you would like your response considered for public inclusion, say so and tell us the name or pseudonym you would want displayed.

Messages are read before any possible publication. Nothing is posted publicly without your permission, and sending a response does not guarantee publication. Your email address will not be included in a published contribution.

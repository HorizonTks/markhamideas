An open invitation

The Conversation.

You need not agree with Markham. In fact, a thoughtful objection may be the most useful contribution of all.

Bring your questions.

Have you encountered an idea that changes how you understand experience, mind or identity? Found an argument that challenges something here? Noticed a contradiction? We would like to hear it.

Contributions can be philosophical, scientific, personal or playful. Curiosity and care matter more than credentials. Disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.

Correspondence is open

Send a question, a counterargument, or a suggestion for the next conversation.

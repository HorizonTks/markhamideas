The voices behind the inquiry

About Markham.

A conversation can begin with a distinction and lead somewhere neither voice expected.

Meet Markham.

Markham is the conversational figure at the heart of writer Robert E. Miller’s evolving philosophical work. In dialogues with his companion, he entertains questions about experience, mind, consciousness, identity, and participation in the world.

Sometimes mischievous, sometimes provocative, Markham gives ideas a voice that another voice can question. The exchanges leave room for disagreement, discovery, and the ordinary details of a lived moment.

The writer and the work.

Robert E. Miller is the writer behind Markham. This site gathers dialogues, essays, and developing questions in one place, including conversations first shared on Substack.

It offers several ways to enter: read an exchange, explore a central proposition, or bring your own objection. You do not need to accept a theory of consciousness before taking part.

Where to begin.

The Inquiry introduces the distinction between experience and the meanings mind gives it. The Conversations lets you encounter the work through its voices and stories. The Spark explores a particular question within that wider inquiry: what might carry continuity, and what would establish that an experiencer continues?

Your voice belongs here.

Markham is open to thoughtful challenge. Personal observations, careful arguments, questions, and laughter can all move an inquiry forward.

An open door

You do not have to agree with Markham to belong in this conversation.
